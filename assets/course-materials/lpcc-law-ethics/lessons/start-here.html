1. How to Use the Program

  • Use the recommended sequence below as a study plan, not as an access requirement.
  • Download and print approved learner-facing workbooks, practice materials, rationale files, performance tools, flashcards, and toolkits for personal study during your access term.
  • For the strongest diagnostic value, complete each answer-hidden assessment before opening its detailed rationale document, even though learner-facing study files are not sequentially locked.
  • Record your answer, confidence, review flags, and timing before checking rationales.
  • Review all four option rationales. Correct guesses and low-confidence correct answers still identify study needs.
  • Use remediation routes, flashcards, and performance tools to repair the reasoning pattern before retesting.
  • Check current official BBS and examination materials near your test date because laws, regulations, forms, fees, and procedures can change.
NO SEQUENTIAL ACCESS LOCKS
All approved learner-facing study materials should be available from the beginning of enrollment. You may study offline and in the order that best supports your preparation. The program may display a recommended order, but it does not require one resource to be completed before another opens.

3. Assessments and Scores

  • CTA assessments use original one-best-answer educational questions. They are not copied or recalled live examination questions.
  • Attempts are unlimited. You may repeat assessments after remediation.
  • An 80% score may be used as a CTA study-readiness benchmark. It is not the official passing score and does not predict passage.
  • Chapter-test technical scoring may use a 70% completion threshold for that chapter-test activity. That internal setting does not control access to other learner materials and is not an official examination passing score.
  • A score does not determine licensure or examination eligibility. Eligibility questions must be resolved with the BBS.

4. Source and Update Notice

CTA’s August 2026 release uses current California BBS legal and examination sources and the currently operative counseling-ethics source. High-change topics - including telehealth, advertising, supervision, records, privacy, examination administration, and counseling ethics - should be checked again near your own examination date. CTA will update the program when material official requirements or ethics standards change.
