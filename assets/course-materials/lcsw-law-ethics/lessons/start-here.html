1. How to Use the Program

  • Use the recommended sequence below as a study plan, not as an access requirement.
  • Download and print learner-facing workbooks, practice materials, rationale files, performance tools, flashcards, and toolkits for personal study during your access term.
  • For the strongest diagnostic value, complete each answer-hidden assessment before opening its detailed rationale document—even though the files are not locked.
  • Record your answer, confidence, review flags, and timing before checking rationales.
  • Review all four option rationales. Correct guesses and low-confidence correct answers still identify study needs.
  • Use remediation routes, flashcards, and performance workbooks to repair the reasoning pattern before retesting.
  • Check current official BBS and examination materials near your test date because laws, regulations, forms, fees, and procedures can change.
NO SEQUENTIAL ACCESS LOCKS
All approved learner-facing study materials should be available from the beginning of enrollment. You may study offline and in the order that best supports your preparation. The course may display a recommended order, but it does not require you to finish one resource before opening another.

3. Assessments and Scores

  • CTA assessments use original one-best-answer educational questions. They are not copied or recalled live examination questions.
  • Attempts are unlimited. You may repeat an assessment after remediation.
  • An 80% score may be used as a CTA study benchmark. It is not the official passing score and does not predict passage.
  • The official examination includes scored and unscored items and is administered under current BBS/Pearson rules. Use current official materials for scheduling, security, accommodations, and test-day procedures.
  • A score does not determine licensure or examination eligibility. Eligibility questions must be resolved with the BBS.
