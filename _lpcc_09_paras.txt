6. Simulation Administration Controls
Display administration instructions before the learner begins.
Use the approved suggested examination period and break instructions for the current-format form.
Present the case in three sections and prevent return to a prior section after submission when the LMS supports section locking.
Require one answer for every item and preserve confidence ratings when implemented.
Do not expose answer keys, blueprint roles, scored-style labels, or field-test-style labels in the candidate interface.
Preserve the original attempt record, start/end time, unanswered items, and completion status.
Do not describe a CTA practice result as an official passing score or NBCC scaled score.
FORM B CONTROL
Form B contains 11 three-part cases and 143 questions in the current-format edition. The controlled scoring resource reports a full practice score and a current-blueprint practice score. Both remain educational practice measures.

8. Learner Support Boundaries
Provide help with access, download, navigation, playback, captions, account issues, and locating the correct resource.
Do not request or accept confidential client information, protected health information, passwords, or full payment-card data in routine support.
Do not determine licensing eligibility, interpret an official accommodation decision, schedule the examination, or guarantee passage.
Refer learners to current official sources for eligibility, fees, testing policies, identification, accommodations, and rescheduling.
Document recurring technical or content issues for quality review and version updates.
