Program Description
The CTA LCSW ASWB Clinical Exam Preparation Program is a complete self-paced system for ASWs, LCSW candidates, and other eligible examinees preparing for the ASWB Clinical Social Work Licensing Examination.
The program teaches candidates to combine social work values, self-determination, person-in-environment assessment, clinical judgment, service planning, intervention, advocacy, and systems reasoning when several answers appear plausible.
Content and simulations reflect the redesigned examination structure used for testing on or after August 3, 2026, including three content areas and a mixture of three- and four-option questions.
What Candidates Will Build
Identify FIRST, NEXT, BEST, MOST, INITIAL, and PRIMARY action cues.
Sequence engagement, assessment, planning, intervention, evaluation, referral, and follow-up.
Apply social work values, self-determination, social justice, and person-in-environment reasoning.
Distinguish the best response from answers that are plausible but premature, overly restrictive, outside scope, or insufficiently collaborative.
Use complete rationales and error logs to repair reasoning rather than memorize answer choices.
Build pacing and stamina under the current 122-question examination structure.

How the Program Works
Complete one LCSW workbook and its applied learning activities.
Take the matching 17-question bank before reviewing the rationales.
Analyze every option, record reasoning errors, and revisit weak concepts.
Continue through all 12 workbooks using the selected study schedule.
Use the Workbook 12 mini-mock for integrated review.
Take 2026 Comprehensive Simulation Form A under timed, closed-book conditions.
Complete targeted remediation using the score profile and rationales.
Take 2026 Comprehensive Simulation Form B as the final readiness assessment.
CTA Learning Model
PREVIEW → LEARN → APPLY → ANALYZE → REPAIR → REVISITCandidates preview the exam task, learn structured content, apply it in original questions, analyze complete rationales, repair reasoning errors, and revisit weak areas before comprehensive simulation.

Frequently Asked Questions
Does this program provide continuing education credit?
No. It is an examination-preparation program and does not provide CE hours or a CE certificate.
Is this program only for California candidates?
The program is designed around the national ASWB Clinical exam and is appropriate for eligible candidates in jurisdictions using that examination. California ASW/LCSW candidates are a primary audience.
Does it reflect the August 2026 ASWB changes?
Yes. The controlling structure uses 122 total questions, 110 scored, 12 unscored, three content areas, mixed three- and four-option questions, and a four-hour limit.
How many full simulations are included?
Two separate 122-question forms are included. Simulation rationales remain controlled until the associated form is submitted.
Are the materials printable?
Yes. The program is designed for online study and printable use.
Does CTA guarantee that I will pass?
No. CTA supports preparation and professional reasoning but cannot guarantee passage or determine exam eligibility.
