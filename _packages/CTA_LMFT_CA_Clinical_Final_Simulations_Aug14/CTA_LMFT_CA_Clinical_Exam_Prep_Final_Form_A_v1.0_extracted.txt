Read the entire vignette and all four options before selecting an answer. Many distractors are clinically plausible but differ in priority, timing, scope, diagnosis, intervention level, or legal/ethical fit.
Choose one response for each item. If two options appear reasonable, select the one that most directly answers the question being asked.
Internal item IDs, blueprint metadata, answer keys, and rationales are intentionally omitted from this learner booklet.
This is an independently developed CTA exam-preparation simulation. It is not an official examination of the California Board of Behavioral Sciences or Pearson VUE.
