
LPCC COMPREHENSIVE SIMULATION - FORM B
143-Question Candidate Examination
Version 2.0 | August 2026 | Current-Format Edition | Exam Preparation Program | No CE Credit
CTA-APPROVED PRODUCTION MASTER - EXTERNAL SME/ETHICS RELEASE GATE PENDING
Form B is a fresh, original CTA simulation designed to approximate the reasoning demands, pacing, and progressive multi-section case format of the current NCMHCE. It is an educational review resource and is not an official NBCC, CCE, Pearson VUE, or California BBS examination.
CTA internal approval recorded August 15, 2026. External SME/ethics validation remains a separate release gate before learner publication.
Administration Instructions
Complete all 11 cases in one sitting when feasible.
Use 225 minutes for the examination questions, with one 15-minute scheduled break after Case 5.
Each case is divided into three sections. In simulation mode, review within the current section only. After advancing, do not return to prior sections or completed cases.
Select one best answer for every question and do not leave items blank.
Record confidence from 1 (guessing) to 5 (high confidence) for later remediation.
Do not open the controlled answer-and-rationales form until the examination is complete.
This is the current-format edition. CTA must review and update the simulation before use with examinations administered under the revised NCMHCE specifications effective July 1, 2027.
Form B contains 11 three-part cases and 143 questions. The controlled scoring map contains 100 scored-style items distributed to the current NCMHCE domains and 43 field-test-style items. Field-test-style items are not identified in the candidate form; answer every item.
